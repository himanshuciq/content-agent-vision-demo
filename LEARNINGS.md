# Learnings (for Himanshu)

Plain-language lessons from building the Ally demo. The full systems-thinking entries live in `~/.claude/MY_LEARNINGS.md`.

## Product and design
- **Lead with the action, back it with proof.** Execs act on "$740K expires in 18 days" and trust it because the delivered section shows how past numbers were measured.
- **Altitude by persona.** The same money shows up three ways: Claire sees buckets, Mike sees batches, the results page sees SKUs. They must add up, or trust breaks at the first click.
- **A tag on a total is a promise about every part.** Label the part it's true for, or change the parts.
- **Benchmark next to actual.** "13% on autopilot" means nothing until "best in class 40%" sits beside it, with the button that closes the gap.
- **The method sets the columns.** A category comparison has to show the benchmark and what it adjusted for. An A/B test shows confidence, because the control is built in.
- **Signed means change.** "+$500K" reads as extra value, and "$500K" reads as revenue. Color it green only once it's real.

## Copy
- **Say it the way a person would.** Fact, then the gap, then the reassurance.
- **Every percentage needs its dollar figure.** A percentage alone hides the size.
- **Results are outcomes.** "Outgrew the category" is a result; "New title live" is only an action.
