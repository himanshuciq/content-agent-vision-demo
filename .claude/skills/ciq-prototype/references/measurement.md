# Measuring and showing value

How the Ally demo proves what each agent delivered. Show the method next to every number; an exec will ask "how did you get that?"

## Language

- **Projected vs delivered,** not promised. Own the gap: every miss has a reason and an amount, and the amounts add up to the gap. Example: $580K − $35K waiting − $10K team input − $15K stockout = $520K.
- **Close with the learning loop:** "25 of 205 SKUs underperformed. Ally tuned itself on what didn't work to make the next run better."
- **Bullet tones:** orange = waiting or short (offer the action on hover: Increase autopilot or Nudge team), red = lost, green = worked, sparkle = learned.

## Content: seasonal work (method tag "vs category")

Event traffic can't be split, so measure against the category.

- **SKU growth** vs **category demand growth** over the same weeks: event weeks vs the 4 weeks before. Category impressions are the demand signal.
- **Lead vs usual:** the SKU's lead over the category, judged against its lead in the previous period. That way a SKU that was already outgrowing the category gets no credit (a difference-in-differences comparison).
- **Adjusted for** price change, ad spend change and days out of stock. Show only the factors that moved; material ones are amber. Incremental sales are after normalizing for them.
- **Table:** SKU · Result (outcome) · SKU growth vs category demand growth (two bars on one scale) · Lead vs usual (verdict color, "usually +X pts" underneath) · Adjusted for · Incremental sales.

## Content: foundational and retail readiness (method tag "A/B tested")

- New content vs old on a 50/50 traffic split. Say that both halves see the same price, ads and stock, so nothing needs adjusting. That heads off the objection before anyone raises it.
- **Table:** SKU · Result (Won A/B test / Lost, back to old content) · Lift vs old content (verdict color) · Confidence · Test length · Incremental sales.
- **Summary:** lift, tests won ("38 of 45"), average confidence.
- The baseline moves after each A/B test. The next test's expected lift is measured against today's content, not the original.

## Ops (method tag "Leakage prevented")

- **Revenue leakage prevented:** each SKU's normal daily sales × the days sooner it was fixed (48 hours instead of about 2 weeks), adjusted for price and seasonality.
- **Break it down by issue type:** buy box, promo badge, shipping speed.

## Media

- IROAS against plan (for example "$3.05 vs $2.80 plan").

## Autopilot

- Share of each workstream's open money that runs itself, against best in class. For example, content is at 13% where best in class is 35%. Use real benchmarks or label them illustrative.

## Mock data honesty

Anything invented for the demo (benchmarks, category growth, "usually" leads, splits across issue types) should be flagged to Himanshu as mock before it goes in front of a customer.
